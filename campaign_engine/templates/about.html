🧠 Machine Learning & NLP Pipeline

  • Sentiment Polarity: Hybrid rule-based & VADER classifier with support for Roman Urdu phrases (*"paisa wasool"*, *"kya product hai"*, *"bekaar"*).
  • Audience Clustering: TF-IDF feature extraction combined with Truncated SVD dimensionality reduction and K-Means clustering.
  • Anomaly Detection: Isolation Forest algorithm to detect engagement anomalies, viral spikes, and irregular comment velocity.
  • Response Curves: Concave power-law regressions (Revenue = a · Spendb) with bisection marginal return solver.
